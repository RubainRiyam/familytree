How a family gets started

  1. One person starts the family

    Usually the secretary or whoever keeps the register. They name the family, add their own household, and get a family code.

  2. They share the code

    On WhatsApp, on the notice for the next meet, or over the phone. Relatives join with their mobile number and the code.

  3. Each home adds its own people

    Every household fills in its own branch. Admins approve, tidy duplicates, and print the directory when it is ready.

Questions families ask

Who can see our family's details?

Only people who are members of your family on Aalthara. Nothing is visible to the public or to search engines. Each person chooses whether their phone number is shown to the family, only to admins, or to nobody.

My mother does not use apps. Can she still be in the tree?

Yes. Anyone can be added by a relative without having the app. If she later signs in with her own number, the profile becomes hers and only she can change it.

What about children?

Children under 18 cannot have an account. A parent can add a child to the tree with a name and birthday. Aalthara does not keep a child's phone number or address, and never shows a child anything based on tracking.

I am in my wife's family association too. Do I need two accounts?

No. One mobile number, one account, one profile. You simply appear in each family you belong to, and each family sees only its own members.

Do you record caste or religion?

No. There is no field for either, and we do not want that information. Families of every community use the same app.

Can we print the directory?

Yes. Admins can download the directory as a PDF, laid out for printing as a booklet, and as a spreadsheet.

What happens if we stop using Aalthara?

Download your data, then ask us to delete the family. We erase it from our systems and from backups within 35 days.

When a child turns 18 they may claim the profile and take control of it.

We share personal data only with service providers that process it for us under contract: cloud database and file hosting, website hosting, the text-message and WhatsApp services that deliver sign-in codes and invitations, Apple and Google for push notifications, our payment processor, and error-monitoring tools. Family data is stored on servers in India. Some providers (for example those that deliver messages or notifications) may process limited data outside India, only as Indian law permits.

  • We keep your account and profile while your account is open.
  • If you have not signed in for three years, we will tell you at least 48 hours in advance and then erase your account unless you sign in.
  • When you or a family ask for erasure, we remove the data from live systems promptly and from backups within 35 days.
  • Payment and tax records are kept for as long as tax and company law require, and access logs for at least one year as the DPDP law requires.

9. Your rights and choices

In the app, go to Me › Privacy and my data, or write to us. You can:

  • See a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who it is shared with, and download a copy.
  • Correct or complete anything in your profile.
  • Withdraw consent and erase your account. See "What erasure does" below.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or are unable to.
  • Complain to our Grievance Officer. We answer within 7 days and resolve complaints within 30 days wherever we can, and in every case within the 90 days the law allows. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

What erasure does

Your contact details, photo, birthday, places, work and every other personal field are deleted and your account is closed. If other people are connected through you in a family tree (for example your children), an unnamed "Private member" placeholder stays in your place so their links to each other are not broken. If nobody is connected through you, the profile is deleted completely.

10. How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Every request to the database is checked against the rule that you can read only families you belong to. Admin actions are logged. Sign-in is by one-time code, so there is no password to steal. We test our access rules automatically before each release. If a personal data breach occurs, we will tell every affected person and the Data Protection Board without delay, and give the Board a detailed report within 72 hours.
